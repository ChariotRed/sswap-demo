Monthly sharing fellowship

Not your average get-together

Our monthly sharing fellowship is a celebration of connection, laughter, and encouragement. We know you need a break, so come as you are: there’s food, and childcare is free.

  • WhenFourth Thursdays, 6–8 pm
  • WherePatterson Park Community Center
  • WhoSingle mothers
  • ChildcareFree, up to age 13

521 Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd, Murfreesboro, TN 37130. Dates may change for special events, so please register.
