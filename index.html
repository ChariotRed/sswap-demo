About us

A community where no mom has to go it alone

Sisters Sharing With A Purpose (S.W.A.P.) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that connects single mothers with community resources through monthly gatherings, social engagement, and activities. Our fellowship is built on caring, trust, confidentiality, empowerment, and love.

Single mothers often carry a lot on their own. We build community so moms feel less isolated, learn what resources are available, get past the barriers to using them, and grow a network of support.

We're based in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, and work alongside Murfreesboro City Schools, Greenhouse Ministries, Patterson Park Community Center, Middle Tennessee State University, and other organizations, with partners outside the state as well.
