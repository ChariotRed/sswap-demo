Rutherford County Wellness Council

Your voice matters: share your story

The Rutherford County Wellness Council wants to uplift the experiences of women and families who have given birth in Rutherford County.

This work is done in partnership with Sisters Sharing With A Purpose, the Tennessee Health Care Campaign, and the Rutherford County Health Department. Sharing these stories builds awareness of the community factors that affect infant mortality.

  • You are 18 or older and gave birth in Rutherford County in the last five years
  • Share your story in person at Patterson Park Community Center (by appointment)
  • About 1 hour, in a private and respectful space
  • Participants receive a gift card

Call or text (615) 949-8443, or email info@sisterssharingwithapurpose.com.
